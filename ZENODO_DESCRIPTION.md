# Rounding and Statistical Simulation

This deposit accompanies the complete English research manuscript *Rounding and Statistical Simulation* by Samir Bouftass. It includes four self-contained papers: *Affine Maps Compatible with Modular Rounding*; *Noise and Simulation of Rounded Observations*; *Dense and Toeplitz Matrices under Signed Row Permutations*; and *Repeated Rounded Observations*.

The materials include readable PDFs, editable sources, exact verification programs, separate numerical optimization checks, recorded results, computation environments, and instructions for reproduction. The thesis treats affine compatibility with finite modular rounding, noisy channels, distribution conversion under signed row permutations, repeated observations of one hidden state, and simulation objectives in two experiments. Each result specifies its hypotheses and the view whose distribution must be preserved. These results do not establish a cryptographic security level or a general hardness reduction.

Samir Bouftass directed the research and is the sole named human author.
