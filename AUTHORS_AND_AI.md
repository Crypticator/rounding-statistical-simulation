# Authorship and use of generative AI

Samir Bouftass directed this research and is the sole named human author of the current thesis and four papers. The documents and supporting programs were developed with extensive assistance from ChatGPT (OpenAI), including mathematical exploration, the formulation and examination of arguments, proof drafting, writing and English translation, generation of verification programs, computational checks, and AI-assisted critical review. This assistance extends beyond language editing.

ChatGPT is identified as an AI tool, not as an author or metadata contributor. The thesis and each paper contain their own disclosure, so the publications remain self-contained. AI-assisted reviews are not independent human peer review. Numerical checks and review reports do not replace mathematical proofs or certify cryptographic security.

Responsibility for the final manuscripts, the accuracy of their claims and the decision to publish rests with the human author. This disclosure does not certify that every argument has been independently checked by a human reviewer.

The authorship revisions are dated 18 November 2026. The underlying mathematical text, verification algorithms and recorded results are preserved. Historical review reports and evidence in the thesis package retain their original attribution and wording. They describe the earlier workflow and are not the current publication metadata.
